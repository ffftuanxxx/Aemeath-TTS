How are the systems compared?
Each example uses the same raw input across systems. Text frontends are labeled: IndexTTS2 with TN on/off, CosyVoice3 and VoxCPM2 with TN off, FireRedTTS3 with WeText, and XTTS-v2 with its native frontend. Systems retain their native voices and decoding configurations.

        
What do the reference and CER mean?
The reference provides human-annotated accepted readings. Forms inside [A|B|C] are alternatives; [X] is optional. CER is calculated from Qwen3-ASR transcripts using case-insensitive normalization and accepted readings. Use the audio players to compare pronunciation, reading completeness and voice quality.

        
How can I explore the audio?
All 180 audio clips are original model outputs. Use Quick A/B to compare two systems on the same input, or download individual WAV files. Sample texts, accepted readings, ASR transcripts and audio paths are available in the listening manifest.
