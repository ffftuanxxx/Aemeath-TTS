What is held constant?
Each case preserves the same benchmark sample ID and raw input across systems. Native frontends are labeled: IndexTTS2 with TN on/off, CosyVoice3 and VoxCPM2 with TN off, FireRedTTS3 with WeText, and XTTS-v2 with its native frontend. The corrected CosyVoice3 rerun is used. Architectures, voices and native decoding configurations differ.

        
What do the reference and CER mean?
The reference is human-annotated. Forms inside [A|B|C] are alternatives; [X] is optional. Cached CER uses blind Qwen3-ASR, case-insensitive normalization and permitted readings. ASR can make mistakes; a zero CER is not a human certification of flawless speech. Listen to the original waveform when comparing pronunciation, completeness and voice quality.

        
Are these the original audio files?
Yes. All 180 WAV files are byte-for-byte copies from completed accuracy runs. No clipping, denoising, loudness adjustment, regeneration or time stretching was applied. The examples come from the previously defined 9,415-item diagnostic subset. Sample IDs, source paths, file hashes, selection rules and cached scores are included in the provenance record and per-clip scores.
